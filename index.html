Welcome to North Star Bakery

Since our first batch of sourdough came out of the oven, North Star Bakery has been the neighborhood's go-to spot for fresh, handmade bread and pastries. Stop in, say hello, and let us know what you're celebrating — we love baking for our neighbors.

What We're Known For

North Star Bakery is known for slow-fermented sourdough, buttery croissants baked fresh every morning, and custom celebration cakes made with local, seasonal ingredients. Everything is mixed, shaped, and baked by hand in our neighborhood kitchen.

Hours & Location

Open Tuesday through Sunday, 7:00 AM – 3:00 PM. Find us at 142 Maple Street, just off the main square — look for the string lights out front.

Featured Item of the Week

This week we're featuring our cinnamon swirl babka — a soft, buttery loaf swirled with cinnamon sugar and finished with a dusting of powdered sugar. Available while supplies last.
