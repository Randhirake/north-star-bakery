Our Story

How North Star Bakery Started

North Star Bakery opened five years ago in a small corner storefront, with one oven and a handful of regulars. Word spread about the sourdough, and today we're proud to be part of the daily routine for so many of our neighbors — whether that's a morning coffee and croissant or a cake for a family celebration.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We source our flour from a family-run mill two hours north of the city, and we buy eggs, butter, and seasonal fruit from local farms whenever we can. We believe good bread starts with good ingredients, and we like knowing where ours come from.

Meet the Bakers

Our team is a small group of dedicated bakers who show up before sunrise to make sure everything is fresh when the doors open. Many of us started as regulars before joining the team behind the counter.
